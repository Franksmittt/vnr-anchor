Debtors / Customers

  1. Create Quotation / Estimate / Pro-forma
  2. Receive Purchase Order from customer
  3. Create Invoice / Tax invoice
  4. Receive & allocate Receipts
  5. Create monthly Statement

Creditors / Suppliers

  1. Receive Quotation / Estimate
  2. Create and send Purchase Order
  3. Capture Supplier invoice / Tax invoice
  4. Pay & allocate Payments
  5. If on account, reconcile monthly Statement

↓ All money flows through the cashbook

Cashbook

Bank, Petty Cash, Credit Card, Loan accounts, Employee reimbursement, etc.

Allocate debtor/customer receipts to the right customer
Allocate creditor/supplier payments to the right supplier
Other income → allocate to the correct GL account (e.g. Interest received)
Other expense → allocate to the correct GL account (e.g. Bank charges, Fuel)
