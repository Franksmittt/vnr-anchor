# VNR — Less noise. Social ad prompts

Reusable prompts for AI image generation (9:16 vertical, 1080×1920).

**Campaign:** Less noise.  
**Style:** fluc-style conceptual ads — one object metaphor, bold type, quiet closer, lots of whitespace.  
**Do not use:** “builders”, service lists, megahertz/features, purple neon, busy collages.

**Locked closer on every ad:** `VNR. Less noise.`
