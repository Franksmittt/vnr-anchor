1. WHERE THE PROJECT IS NOW
================================================================================

This repository is the VNR Professional Accountants marketing and lead-generation
website. VNR is presented as a Centurion-headquartered accounting, tax,
compliance, and wealth-structuring partner serving clients across South Africa.

Recent UI and content updates (2026):
- Slimmer homepage with a static full-viewport hero and compact preview sections
  for services, team, and insights
- Header shows the VNR logo with "Professional Accountants" beside it
- Services/pricing page uses category sidebar navigation, in-category search,
  sorting, and clean name + price rows (internal SKU codes are not shown on site)
- Homepage authority bar shows SAIPA, Dext, and Sage partner logos on white tiles
- Contact page uses equal-height form/details columns and a working Google Maps
  embed (CSP updated for map iframes)
- Footer includes Endpoint Media credit and Facebook social link
- Mobile responsiveness pass across header, services, contact, insights, and
  shared components
- User-facing copy cleaned to avoid em dash punctuation

Current implementation highlights:
- Next.js App Router application with 40 implemented app pages:
  - 15 static/support pages
  - 8 service detail pages
  - 17 insight article pages
- Homepage, services/pricing, insights, contact, Anchor Wealth, expat guide,
  local Centurion/Pretoria landing page, legal pages, and internal email
  signature generator are implemented.
- Services/pricing page uses the July 2026 price list from data/pricing-data.ts
  with category filtering, keyword search, sort options, VAT-inclusive pricing,
  "FREE", and quote labels.
- Service and insight pages are generated from structured data files.
- SEO foundations are in place: metadata helpers, canonical production domain,
  dynamic sitemap, environment-aware robots, structured data, breadcrumbs, and
  noindex directives for legal/internal/conversion pages where appropriate.
- Middleware handles lowercase URL normalization (excluding static image paths),
  selected legacy redirects, canonical host redirect from www.vnr.co.za to
  vnr.co.za, one 410 deprecated path, and baseline security headers.
- Contact form posts to app/api/contact/route.ts, validates required fields,
  tracks successful submissions, and redirects to /thank-you. The API currently
  acknowledges submissions only; no email, CRM, or database delivery is wired in
  from the visible code.
- Analytics hooks are GA4-ready when NEXT_PUBLIC_GA_MEASUREMENT_ID is provided.
  Tracked interactions include WhatsApp, phone, email, social clicks, and form
  submit.
- Automated SEO regression coverage exists through Playwright and a GitHub
  Actions SEO gate.

Known handover notes:
- The live/contact delivery destination should be confirmed before production
  launch because the current contact API does not persist or forward enquiries.
- Deployment operations still need to be completed outside code: domain/DNS,
  SSL, production environment variables, Google Search Console verification, and
  sitemap submission.
- The legacy redirect map still includes /who-we-are -> /team, but no /team page
  exists in the current app tree. Review that redirect before launch if legacy
  traffic is expected.

2. VNR SERVICES OFFERED
================================================================================

Primary service pages:

1. Secretarial Services
   Route: /services/secretarial-services
   Covers CIPC annual returns, director/member changes, registered office
   maintenance, statutory records, company amendments, and compliance support.

2. Payroll Administration
   Route: /services/payroll-administration
   Covers monthly payroll processing, payslips, EMP201 and EMP501 submissions,
   UIF, COID, letters of good standing, and payroll compliance support.

3. Tax Advisory
   Route: /services/tax-advisory
   Covers personal and corporate income tax, provisional tax, VAT, PAYE, SDL,
   UIF, SARS audits, objections, dispute resolution, and tax planning.

4. Business Structuring
   Route: /services/business-structuring
   Covers new company registrations, NPO/NPC guidance, entity selection, trust
   formation, asset protection, and long-term structuring.

5. Financial Reporting
   Route: /services/financial-reporting
   Covers annual financial statements, management accounts, tax calculations,
   IFRS for SMEs reporting, and independent review support.

6. Confirmations
   Route: /services/confirmations
   Covers income confirmations, audit reports for property purchases, BBBEE
   declarations, and other verification documents.

7. Estate & Legacy Planning
   Route: /services/legacy-planning
   Covers wills, intergenerational wealth transfer, estate duty planning,
   professional executor services, trust and estate administration, and business
   continuity planning.

8. Cloud Accounting & Financial Record Keeping
   Route: /services/cloud-accounting
   Covers Sage Business Cloud, Xero, Dext, cloud accounting setup, data
   migration, software training, workflow automation, and bookkeeping/record
   keeping solutions.

Services and pricing categories in the July 2026 price list:
- Tax Advisory & Compliance
- Financial Reporting
- Payroll Administration
- Registrations & Secretarial Services
- Estate & Legacy Planning
- Confirmations
- Business Structuring
- Cloud Accounting & Financial Record Keeping Solutions

Additional advisory channels:
- Anchor Wealth Division: investment structuring routed through Anchor Capital,
  with VNR retaining tax strategy and compliance oversight.
- Independent advisor desk: life cover, disability cover, dread disease cover,
  buy-and-sell planning, key person cover, share buy-back funding, and short-term
  insurance reviews.
- Estate & Will Clinic: will drafting/refresh support when VNR or an approved
  executor partner is appointed.
- Expat and contractor tax guidance: residency, foreign income, provisional tax,
  double-tax relief, and SARS compliance support.
