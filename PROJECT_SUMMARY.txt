1. WHERE THE PROJECT IS NOW
================================================================================

This repository is the VNR Professional Accountants marketing and lead-generation
website. VNR is presented as a Centurion-headquartered accounting, tax,
compliance, and wealth-structuring partner serving clients across South Africa.

The site includes 48 public-facing routes (10 core landing pages, 8 service
detail pages, 17 insight articles, 3 team profiles, plus legal and conversion
pages), a password-protected back office for internal tools, and five API
endpoints. Content is driven from structured TypeScript data files with SEO
metadata, structured data, sitemap generation, and Playwright SEO regression
tests.

Recent updates (June 2026):
- Header navigation: Risk & Estate dropdown (Estate & Will Clinic, Independent
  Advisor Desk); Anchor Wealth uses exact-path active state; duplicate Contact
  link removed (Get in Touch CTA retained); dropdown hover bridge and close
  delay for better usability
- WhatsApp: +27 76 816 5533 (wa.me/27768165533) with official logo icon
- Contact form: Resend email delivery wired (returns 503 until API key and
  domain are configured in Vercel)
- Back office (/back-office): password-protected hub with Email Signatures tab
  and Service Prices tab; live prices stored in Vercel Blob (private store)
- Public /services page reads live prices from Blob with fallback to
  data/pricing-data.ts
- /email-signatures redirects to /back-office?tab=signatures
- Pricing display: excl. VAT shown bold, incl. VAT shown below
- Blog authors aligned to client assignments across all 17 insight articles
- Slim homepage, responsive layout pass, partner logos (SAIPA, Dext, Sage),
  Google Maps embed on contact page, Endpoint Media footer credit

Known handover notes:
- DNS cutover to Vercel in progress: update A/CNAME in cPanel only; keep MX,
  SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records untouched for email
- Resend requires RESEND_API_KEY plus verified vnr.co.za domain in Vercel
- Legacy redirect /who-we-are -> /team exists, but there is no /team index
  page (only /team/[slug] profiles). Consider adding a team listing page or
  redirecting to homepage if legacy traffic is expected
